Privacy Policy & Play Store Data Safety

Transparency, security, and student data protection standards.

1. Overview & Educational Purpose

Chatty is designed as a precision AI study companion for students and researchers in Nanotechnology, Biotechnology, AI/ML, Cognitive Science, and related STEM disciplines.

2. Data Collection & Firebase Storage

  • Account Credentials: When you sign in with your Google Account, we store your unique User ID (UID), display name, and email address to authenticate your session and sync your data.
  • Study Conversations & Notes: Chat transcripts, formula inquiries, and practice problem sets are saved to Google Firebase Firestore under encrypted, user-isolated security rules (`request.auth.uid == userId`).
  • Attachments & Papers: Documents uploaded for study analysis are processed transiently in memory for context ingestion.
  • Zero Ad Tracking: We do NOT sell student data, do NOT use advertising trackers, and do NOT share personal information with third-party data brokers.

3. Generative AI Safety & Content Disclaimer

Chatty utilizes Google Gemini 3.7 Flash generative reasoning models. While designed for high accuracy in STEM problem solving:

  • AI responses are intended for study assistance and conceptual exploration. Students must verify high-stakes examination answers and lab calculations with primary academic sources.
  • You can report any inaccurate or inappropriate model response using the in-message report tool.

4. User Rights & Google Play Account Deletion

In full compliance with Google Play Store User Data policies, you hold full ownership of your data. You may:

  • Delete individual chat sessions or all history directly from the sidebar.
  • Permanently delete your account and wipe all stored cloud documents from your Profile Settings at any time with a single tap.

5. Contact & Compliance

For questions or data deletion requests, contact the development team at rixieplayz1@gmail.com.
